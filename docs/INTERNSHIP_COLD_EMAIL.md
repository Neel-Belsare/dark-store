## 🎯 Target Companies & Outreach Strategy

### 1. Types of Companies to Target:
* **Regional & Tier-2 Quick-Commerce**: Country Delight, Fraazo, Otipy, Milkbasket, Kisaan Konnect, Dunzo Daily, Pluckk, CityMall.
* **Campus & Hyperlocal Delivery Startups**: Late-night delivery apps, college campus delivery networks, micro-grocery startups.
* **D2C Rapid Delivery Brands**: Niche meat/seafood (Licious, FreshToHome), gourmet foods, rapid pharmacy delivery.
* **Logistics & Dark Store SaaS**: Increff, Unicommerce, Shipsy, Locus.sh, Shadowfax, Porter.

### 2. Best Practices for High Reply Rates:
1. **Find Founders/CTOs on LinkedIn**: Look for "Co-Founder", "CTO", "Head of Engineering", or "Lead Architect".
2. **Personalize the First Line**: Mention their recent city expansion, product update, or a blog post they wrote.
3. **Attach Visuals**: Include the 1-page architecture flowchart (`docs/screenshots/architecture_flowchart.png`) as a direct attachment or inline image.
4. **Follow Up in 3-4 Days**: A polite 2-sentence follow-up doubles reply rates.
